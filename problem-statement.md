Official Problem Statement

“Turn the Real World into a Game.”

 

What if the world around you could become part of a game?

Your phone camera, location, voice, movement, surroundings, maps, sensors, and even live real-world data can all become part of the experience. With AI, these everyday things can be transformed into characters, missions, challenges, stories, or entire game worlds.

For KICKR CODEMANIA 2026, your challenge is simple:

Build a playable game that connects the real world with AI.

But there’s one important rule:

AI must actually matter.

It shouldn’t just be a chatbot sitting inside your game or an extra feature added at the end. AI should actively influence how the game works—how players make decisions, how challenges change, how characters behave, how the story develops, or how the game responds to the player.

The goal is to create something where the real world changes the way the game is played.

1. THE CHALLENGE

You have 24 hours to build a working game prototype that brings together:

REAL WORLD + AI + GAMEPLAY = A NEW GAMING EXPERIENCE

The big question is:

How can AI turn something from the real world into an engaging game?

You decide what that looks like.

It could be a game that uses your camera to understand the world around you, your location to create missions, your voice to interact with characters, your movement to control progression, or live data to change the game.

The possibilities are wide open.

REAL-WORLD INSPIRATION

Here are some ideas to get you thinking.

These are examples, not fixed problem statements.

You are completely free to come up with something different.

1. 📍 Location

Turn the player's surroundings into a treasure hunt.

AI could generate quests based on nearby landmarks, streets, buildings, or locations.

2. 📷 Camera

Point your phone at something in the real world.

Computer vision could identify what the player sees and AI could turn it into a character, weapon, resource, enemy, or challenge.

3. 🎙️ Voice

Let players talk directly to AI-powered characters.

Characters could understand what players say, remember previous conversations, and react differently based on their choices.

4. 🏃 Movement & Fitness

Turn walking, running, or physical movement into gameplay.

AI could create new challenges based on how the player moves or performs.

5. 🌦️ Real-World Data

Use weather, traffic, public information, or other live data to change the game.

For example, a change in real-world weather could affect the game world, missions, enemies, or difficulty.

6. 🗺️ Maps

Turn a real city into your game map.

Players could explore real locations while completing AI-generated quests, battles, puzzles, or missions.

7. 👥 Social Interaction

Bring other people into the experience.

AI could create teams, missions, challenges, or consequences based on how players interact with each other.

8. 🌱 Environment

Use the player's surroundings as part of the game.

Players could photograph or identify real-world environmental conditions, while AI converts them into missions, points, challenges, or rewards.

9. 🎓 Education

Turn learning into a game.

AI could act as an adaptive game master, changing questions, challenges, and difficulty based on how the player performs.

10. 👀 Surroundings

Make the physical environment the game world.

AI could understand what the camera sees and dynamically create gameplay around the player's surroundings.

The key idea:

Don't just collect real-world data and display it inside a game.

Make the real world actually change the way the game is played.

IMPORTANT: AI MUST MATTER

This is one of the most important things the judges will look for.

Ask yourself:

“What happens if I remove the AI from my game?”

If the answer is:

“Almost nothing changes.”

Then the AI probably isn't doing enough.

The same applies to your real-world component.

Using GPS, a camera, or live data just for the sake of saying you used it isn't enough.

The technology should have a purpose.

The real world should influence the gameplay.

AI should make the experience smarter, more dynamic, more personal, or simply more fun.
